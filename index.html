How ASH//FLOW works

ASH//FLOW helps you find tracks that mix well together, play them through Spotify, and turn a night of listening into a playlist.

The Camelot wheel explained

The wheel is a map of musical keys arranged so that keys which sound good together sit next to each other. DJ Mark Davis created it as a simpler way to use the musicians' "circle of fifths", and Mixed In Key popularised it. Spotify's Mix view, Rekordbox and most DJ software use the same codes.

The numbers: 1 to 12 around the clock

There are 12 notes in music, so there are 12 positions. Each step clockwise moves the key up by a "fifth", the most natural-sounding move in music. Neighbouring numbers share 6 of their 7 notes, which is why they blend smoothly. Numbers wrap around: 12 sits next to 1.

The letters: A and B

  • A = minor: darker, moodier, more tension. The inner ring.
  • B = major: brighter, happier, more open. The outer ring.
  • The same number in A and B (like 8A, A minor, and 8B, C major) uses exactly the same 7 notes, just centred differently. That's why the mood switch works.

The colours

The colour only tells you the number. Each number has its own colour, arranged in rainbow order around the wheel, so neighbouring numbers have neighbouring colours. 8A and 8B share a colour. Colours that sit close together on the wheel usually mix well; colours from opposite sides usually clash.

These are ASH//FLOW's colours. Spotify's Mix view and other apps use their own colour sets, so the same key can be a different colour there. The code (like 7B) is what matters, not the colour.

What the wheel shows you

  • White outline: the key you've selected, or the key of the track you're mixing out of.
  • Bright colours: keys that mix well with it, using the moves you have switched on.
  • Dark grey: keys that don't fit. Mixing into these will usually clash.
  • The small number under each key (like Am · 188) is the key's name and how many of your tracks are in it.
  • Tap the centre to show every track again.

The moves, safest first

Same key8A → 8AAll 7 notes shared. Always works.
One step8A → 7A or 9A6 of 7 notes shared. Smooth; +1 lifts slightly, −1 settles.
Mood switch8A → 8BAll 7 notes shared, new centre. Minor to major brightens; major to minor darkens.
Diagonal8A → 9B, 8B → 7A6 of 7 notes shared, plus a mood change. Colourful but still works.
Energy boost8A → 10A5 of 7 notes shared. Raises the key a whole step for a noticeable lift. Use sparingly, ideally on a clean break.

Labels you'll see in lists

  • Pink capitals above a track (like ONE STEP) tell you which move gets you there from the track you're mixing out of.
  • Key clash in amber, in your queue, means that transition breaks the rules. It might still work over a drum break, but listen first.
  • ? means the track has no key in your library yet.
  • ★ Played after this in green means you've gone from the track you're mixing out of into that one before, in a flow or a crate you saved. Those float to the top.
  • ✎ next to a title means you've left yourself a note on it. Add one from the ⋯ menu.

Direction

The Direction switch under the wheel tells the app where you want the night to go. Build favours a few tempo up, energy boosts and minor to major. Hold keeps tempo and key close. Cool favours tempo down and major to minor, and avoids boosts. Any just ranks by closest key and tempo. It only changes the order of the wheel list and the suggestions on the Playing tab, never how many tracks you see. It works from the track you're mixing out of, and each row shows the tempo change from it (like +3). Each flow on the Flows tab has a tempo chart so you can see the shape of the night afterwards.

The wheel narrows the options. Tempo, energy, vocals and phrasing still decide whether a blend works, so trust your ears over the colours.

Getting started
  • Open the app from its home-screen icon. To install it again: open ashleyfouche.github.io/key-wheel in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Tap Connect Spotify (top right) and approve. Playback needs Spotify Premium.
  • If a bar says Reconnect Spotify, tap it once. That happens when a new feature needs an extra permission.
The wheel and keys
  • Every key has a number from 1 to 12 and a letter. A is minor (darker), B is major (brighter). The outer ring is B, the inner ring is A.
  • Tap a key to see the keys that mix well with it and your tracks in them. Tap the centre to go back to all tracks.
  • Same key: the safest blend. One step: one number up or down, same letter. Mood switch: same number, other letter (lifts or darkens). Energy boost: two numbers up, use sparingly. Diagonal: minor one up to major, or major one down to minor.
  • Tap a move button to show only that move. Tap it again to show every move.
  • Search always looks through every track, whatever key is selected.
Your thread
  • Tap a track's name to mix out of it. The wheel jumps to its key, the tempo filter sets to 6 either side, and the list shows what fits.
  • Keep tapping tracks to build your thread, the chain of tracks you've moved through.
  • ‹ Back one steps back a track. Tap any track in the thread to jump back to it. Clear thread starts again.
  • Save thread as crate puts the whole thread into your crate in order.
Playing through Spotify
  • ▶ plays a track straight away. + adds it to your crate. ⋯ opens more options: queue, mix out of it, go to the artist or album, or open it in Spotify.
  • Swipe right on any track to add it to your queue.
  • Your queue lives in ASH//FLOW, so you can reorder it and remove tracks, which Spotify doesn't let other apps do. The app hands Spotify one track at a time, so keep ASH//FLOW open while you play. The next track (marked "Sent to Spotify") can't be removed.
  • Music plays on whichever device Spotify is using: your phone, a speaker, AirPods or your laptop. Spotify needs to be open, or recently used, somewhere.
  • The bar at the bottom shows what's playing, with its key. Mix out of this makes it your starting track.
  • Follow: every new track Spotify plays becomes the track you mix out of and joins your thread automatically.
  • The Playing tab shows large artwork, a progress bar you can drag, skip buttons, and Up next, your queue, labelled with how each track mixes from the one before. A clash shows as Key clash.
  • Tap an artist or album from the ⋯ menu to see their tracks and releases, with keys from your library where you have them.
Flows
  • Every track that plays for 30 seconds or more is saved, whether you started it here or in Spotify. Quick skips are left out.
  • Plays are grouped into flows. A break of more than an hour starts a new one.
  • Opening the app also collects your last 50 plays from Spotify, so tracks played while the app was closed still appear.
  • Use as crate starts a fresh crate from a flow. Add to crate adds a flow after what's already there, skipping repeats, so you can combine nights.
Crates and saving to Spotify
  • Add tracks with +, your thread, a flow, or the current list.
  • Drag the ≡ handle to change the order. Tick Sort by tempo to order by tempo when saving.
  • New playlist creates a private Spotify playlist. Add to existing adds to one of your playlists and skips tracks already in it.
  • Spotify doesn't let apps move playlists into folders, so drag them into a folder in Spotify yourself.
Your playlists
  • The Playlists tab shows playlists you own, with keys from your library. Spotify only shares the tracks of playlists you own, so copy anything else into one of yours first.
  • Tracks marked ? don't have a key in your library yet.
Adding keys for new tracks
  1. Put the new tracks in one of your own Spotify playlists, open it on your phone and tap Mix so keys and tempos show.
  2. Screenshot the list as you scroll and send the screenshots to Claude.
  3. Claude replies with lines like 7B | 130 | Positive | Jamback | 2:49. Copy them.
  4. Open that playlist in the Playlists tab, tap Add keys, paste and tap Match. ASH//FLOW pairs each line with the exact track in the playlist. Anything it can't find there, it searches Spotify for, using the length to pick the right version.
  5. Check the list: ✓ is a confident match, ⚠ needs a look (unticked until you tick it), ✕ wasn't found. Tap Save ticked tracks.
  6. For tracks that aren't in a playlist, paste into More → Add tracks instead.
Good to know
  • Your crate, flows and added tracks are stored on this phone. Use More → Back up my data now and then, especially before changing phones.
  • Spotify asks you to log in again about every six months.
  • The wheel and your library work offline. Playing and saving need a connection.
  • Keys come from Spotify's Mix view, so a few may be off by a step. The rules narrow the options; your ears decide the blend.

About the keys

Keys and tempos come from Spotify's Mix view. A is minor, B is major. Same key, one step, mood switch (same number, other letter), energy boost (+2) and diagonal moves all mix cleanly. The rules narrow the field; your ears decide the blend.
